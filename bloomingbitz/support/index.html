BloomingBitz Support

BloomingBitz is developed and operated by Shibil Poulose under the EspeeLabs brand.

For help with BloomingBitz, email ourfamspace@outlook.com.

If you email support, we receive the sender’s email address and the content of the message. We use that information to respond. Support email is handled through Microsoft Outlook.

Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are optional. Please avoid including sensitive information.

Steps are not updating

Check that Motion & Fitness permission is enabled for BloomingBitz in iPhone Settings. In the app, open System and choose Retry Motion Data.

A Bloom must be started or continued to count eligible steps. Pause excludes walking during the paused interval. Locking the phone does not pause a running Bloom.

Recovery depends on the motion history available from iOS. The app reports unavailable coverage rather than estimating steps.

An advertisement will not load

Rewarded advertisements require an internet connection and may be unavailable at times. Follow the displayed message and try again later. Personal walking and saved History remain available offline.

If the app reports that an earned reward could not be saved, keep the app open and use the retry option shown in System. An unsaved reward is not kept if the app is closed before saving succeeds. If a message is already unlocked, you can issue another sharing code from Send a Message without watching another advertisement until that unlock expires.

A sharing code will not work

Check the code and its deadline. Codes are case-insensitive and contain six characters.

Entering a code already redeemed on this installation opens its existing Bloom or History record. Codes that have expired cannot start a new Bloom.

Where is my progress stored?

Progress is stored locally on your device. BloomingBitz has no account or cross-device sync.

Deleting the app removes its local app data from the device. Device backups may retain copies of BloomingBitz records. Restoring a backup may restore those records. Contacting support cannot recover progress from a BloomingBitz server.

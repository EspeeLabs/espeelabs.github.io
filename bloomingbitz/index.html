The story

From a reminder to a reason to walk.

Send a Message

Choose a Bloom. Write. Share.

Pick a design and write a short note. Share the Bloom when you are ready. The words stay hidden until they walk.

  1. 1 · Choose a Bloom

    Thank You, Proud of You, or Love You Always — for family, or for someone else you care about.

  2. 2 · Write a note

    A few personal words. They unlock after the first walking milestone.

3 · Share the Bloom. They open Discover the Message, then walk.

Discover the Message

Open a Bloom. Walk. Reveal.

Paste the invitation and start walking. Artwork gathers with every step. At the end, their message is waiting.

  1. 1 · Discover the Message

    Open the Bloom they sent you.

  2. 2 · Walk to Reveal

    Artwork gathers as they walk.
